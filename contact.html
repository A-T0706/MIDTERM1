Project Reflection
and Final Summary

This project explored how Harlem Plaza could be transformed through stronger landscape design, decorative planting, and more inviting public-space features. AI-generated images helped visualize multiple directions for improving the plaza’s identity and atmosphere.

🔍

Research

The project began by studying the current plaza and identifying its strengths and missed opportunities.

🤖

AI Exploration

AI images were used to test how greenery and decorative design changes could improve the space.

🌼

Design Focus

The redesign emphasized planting, atmosphere, comfort, and public identity.

✅

Outcome

The final proposal presents Harlem Plaza as a greener, more decorative, and more welcoming place.

Project complete

This site combines research, redesign visuals, and final proposal presentation.
