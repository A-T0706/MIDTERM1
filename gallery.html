Gallery

Current site conditions and redesign concepts showing how Montefiore Square could become more comfortable, flexible, and community-centered.

Visual Development

These images compare the current plaza with multiple redesign directions developed during the project. The gallery is meant to show both the existing problems of the site and the design ideas used to respond to them. Instead of focusing only on appearance, each concept explores how planting, seating, layout, and atmosphere can improve comfort, identity, and public use.

Current Plaza View 1

This image shows the current condition of Montefiore Square after its recent renovation. The plaza has open paving, lawn, and newly planted trees, but the trees are still too young to provide strong shade. The space appears clean and organized, yet it still feels exposed and more suited to passing through than staying for long periods of time.

Current Plaza View 2

This view highlights the circulation paths and fixed seating in the existing plaza. While the layout is functional, it offers limited flexibility for people who want to gather, rest, or arrange themselves in different ways. The image helps explain why the redesign focuses on comfort, seating variety, and stronger visual identity.

Concept 1

Concept 1 explores a greener version of the plaza with stronger planting and a softer landscape character. This direction creates a calmer and more inviting atmosphere that could help the space feel less harsh and more restorative. Its strength is environmental comfort, although it may be less focused on social gathering than the other concepts.

Concept 2

Concept 2 emphasizes social use by making the plaza feel more active and more gathering-oriented. The arrangement suggests a stronger focus on seating, movement, and public interaction. This concept is useful because it shows how the plaza could function as a more social neighborhood space, although it still needs a balance between activity and comfort.

Concept 3

Concept 3 is the final selected direction because it combines the strongest parts of the earlier ideas. It keeps a green and welcoming atmosphere while also improving flexibility, comfort, and visual identity. This concept best reflects the project goal of creating a plaza that is not only attractive, but also more usable and more connected to the Hamilton Heights community.
