Montefiore Square Redesign Proposal

Montefiore Square is a triangular public plaza located at the intersection of Broadway, West 138th Street, and Hamilton Place in Hamilton Heights, Manhattan. Although the site was renovated in 2022, important issues remain unresolved. The trees are still too young to provide meaningful shade, seating is limited and inflexible, and the space lacks a strong connection to the neighborhood’s identity. This project proposes a redesign that improves comfort, adds flexible seating, increases shade, and creates a stronger sense of place for the people who use the plaza every day.

Essential Question

How can Montefiore Square be redesigned to improve comfort, safety, identity, and community use for Hamilton Heights residents—not just look better, but function better for the people who rely on it?

🌿

Shade & Cooling

Additional planting and shade structures reduce heat and make the plaza more comfortable during warmer months.

✨

Neighborhood Identity

Design elements like materials, color, and public art reflect the culture and character of Hamilton Heights.

🪑

Flexible Seating

Movable chairs and tables allow people to choose how they sit, gather, and use the space.

🌞

Everyday Use

The redesign supports commuters, students, families, and seniors with a more functional and welcoming layout.

Before

The current plaza has strong infrastructure but lacks shade, flexible seating, and a clear neighborhood identity.

After

The redesign improves comfort, adds flexible seating, increases shade, and creates a stronger connection to the Hamilton Heights community.
