Reimagining Harlem Plaza
as a stronger public space

This project studies the current plaza and presents an AI-assisted redesign proposal. The redesign focuses on adding more greenery, decorative planting, gathering spaces, comfort, and a stronger visual identity for the community.

🌿

More Greenery

Additional planting beds, layered flowers, and more landscape elements make the plaza feel more alive.

✨

Decorative Features

New decorative lighting, planter edges, and focal elements help the plaza feel more memorable and inviting.

🪑

Social Seating

Seating areas support rest, gathering, and everyday public use for different groups in the neighborhood.

🌞

Comfort + Shade

Trees and shaded areas improve comfort and encourage people to spend more time in the space.

Current Conditions

The plaza already has open space and trees, but it can become much more vibrant with richer planting, decorative details, and stronger places for people to gather.

Redesign Vision

The redesign introduces colorful planting, improved atmosphere, decorative features, and a more welcoming public experience while keeping the plaza open and usable.
