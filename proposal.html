Proposal

A redesigned Harlem Plaza with more greenery, decoration, and public comfort.

Final Design Proposal
for Harlem Plaza

The final proposal enhances the plaza by adding decorative flower beds, richer planting, stronger seating zones, and more visually attractive public-space elements. The goal is to make the plaza feel more vibrant, comfortable, and community-centered.

🌺

Decorative Planting

Flowering beds and layered planting create stronger color, texture, and visual identity.

💡

Decorative Elements

Features such as lighting, planters, and enhanced edges create a more designed public atmosphere.

🧍

Gathering Space

The redesign supports staying, gathering, resting, and enjoying the plaza in a more social way.

🏙️

Improved Identity

The plaza becomes not just open space, but a more recognizable and welcoming neighborhood place.

Before

The existing plaza has a solid base, but there is room for stronger atmosphere and more decorative variety.

After

The redesign adds visual richness, stronger planting, and more public comfort while keeping the plaza open and usable.
