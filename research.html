Research

Studying the current plaza and identifying opportunities for improvement.

Site Observations
and Design Questions

The research phase looked at how Harlem Plaza currently functions and how it could be improved. The site already has open space and tree cover, but it has the potential to feel richer, more decorative, and more inviting through targeted design changes.

🌳

Existing Green Base

The plaza already has mature trees and open lawn space, which creates a strong foundation for redesign.

🎨

Need for More Visual Interest

More flowers, planters, and decorative components could help the plaza feel more memorable and lively.

👥

Public Use

The site can support more social activity through clearer gathering zones and better seating areas.

🛠️

Redesign Opportunity

AI-assisted concepts were used to test how decorative planting and comfort features could transform the space.
